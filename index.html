Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 20, 2026

Summary: CulinaConvert is built with privacy in mind. Your recipes, measurement conversions, custom ingredients, pan scaling ratios, and favorites remain 100% on your local device in a private local database.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how CulinaConvert ("we," "our," or "the app") manages information when you use our Android application. We are committed to transparency and safeguarding user privacy.

2. Data Collection and Storage

CulinaConvert is primarily an offline-first culinary density converter and baking utility:

  • Local Data Storage: All user-authored content—such as saved pantry favorites, recent conversion history, custom dough formulas, custom pan dimensions, and custom ingredient densities—is stored strictly locally on your Android device using the Room SQLite database framework.
  • No Cloud Sync or Remote Accounts: We do not require accounts, logins, email verification, or registration to use the app. Your culinary entries are never transmitted, uploaded, or sold to external servers.
  • Full User Control: You can delete any individual favorite, custom ingredient, calculation history record, or wipe all stored data at any time directly through the app settings or individual delete buttons.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services

CulinaConvert is supported by advertising provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process certain non-personal device information to display advertisements, including:

  • IP address and general coarse location (country/city level).
  • Mobile Advertising Identifier (Google Advertising ID / GAID).
  • Device identifiers, operating system version, and technical diagnostic telemetry.

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services

4. Android Permissions

CulinaConvert requests the minimal permissions required for basic functionality:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for the Google Mobile Ads SDK to fetch and render ads.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to check whether an active network connection exists.

The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location GPS, external storage media, or device sensor hardware.

5. Children's Privacy

CulinaConvert does not knowingly collect or solicit any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The app is a general-audience cooking measurement tool.

6. Security

Because your conversion records and favorites are stored locally in application-private storage sandbox on your Android device, your data is protected by the Android operating system's security architecture.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
